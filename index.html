Watch for the difference: Both systems use exactly the same routes. In the left panel agents push forward and may collide; on the right, they pause to let another agent or moving obstacle pass.
This is an illustrative discrete, one-cell-per-step model. Same-cell and edge-swap conflicts are checked. In the safety-policy panel, a moving obstacle may yield as part of the coordinated fleet and occupies one predicted cell per step; it is not an industrial safety guarantee. All agents start on their own maze pixels in both cases. Only feasible subsets of the originally generated agents are displayed. Original maze geometry, agent paths and moving-obstacle patrol tracks are unchanged. Excluded agents are listed; a wait-only rule cannot resolve every arbitrary fixed-route traffic configuration.
